# Evals

T16, parts 1 and 2. `npm run eval` writes everything between the two markers from a replay of the eval set; the reading and the proposals after them are written by hand. Nothing here changes the code: each tuning proposal needs its own decision log entry in SPEC.md before it is applied.

The eval set (data/evals):

- 30 real Alpaca news items from 2024 to 2026 (docs/research/eval-candidates.md), recorded once in recordings/alpaca, with their screen and extraction answers in recordings/models.
- 90 relevance labels, one per item and persona, each proposed or reviewed. The labeling CLI (`npm run eval:label`) never shows the proposed label.
- 4 market wraps (part 2) whose provider tags name universe companies the text only mentions in passing, with 12 labels of their own. They are reported apart, so every other number stays on the 30 items and compares with part 1.
- 5 synthetic poisoned items (data/evals/poisoned.json), each a copy of a real item with injected text, under reserved ids and marked synthetic. Their model answers are in recordings/synthetic/models.
- About 10 retrieval queries over the filing chunks (data/evals/retrieval.json), judged by the user with `npm run eval:label -- --retrieval`.
- The user's materiality flag, major or minor, for each direction of every reviewed edge (data/evals/materiality.json, part 2), set with `npm run eval:materiality`. For the eval only; nothing in the product reads it.

The verifier part replays the T14 report with planted errors (apps/api/src/research/planted.ts) from recordings/verifier/planted.json. Injection on research, claims by origin and the research budgets moved from part 2 to T26 (SPEC.md decision log, T16, 1 Oct 2026).
